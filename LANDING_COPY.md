## FAQ Section

### Headline
"Frequently Asked Questions"

### Q1: Is this really free?
**A**: Yes! The core audit is 100% free forever. No credit card required, no hidden fees. We may offer premium features in the future, but the basic audit will always be free.

### Q2: How accurate are the recommendations?
**A**: Our recommendations are based on official pricing data and conservative estimates. We show our reasoning for every recommendation so you can make informed decisions.

### Q3: Do you store my data?
**A**: We only store what's necessary to generate your audit. We don't store sensitive information like API keys or passwords. Your audit is private by default.

### Q4: Which AI tools do you support?
**A**: We currently support Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, Windsurf, and v0. We're adding more tools regularly.

### Q5: How long does it take?
**A**: The audit takes about 2 minutes to complete. You'll get instant results with personalized recommendations.

### Q6: Can I share my results?
**A**: Yes! Every audit gets a unique shareable URL. You can share it with your team, investors, or anyone else.

### Q7: Do I need to sign up?
**A**: No signup required to get your audit. We only ask for your email if you want to receive the full report and unlock sharing.

### Q8: How do you make money?
**A**: The core audit is free. We plan to offer premium features like historical tracking, team dashboards, and API access in the future.

## Tone & Voice

### Brand Voice
- **Knowledgeable**: We know AI tools and pricing
- **Supportive**: We're here to help, not judge
- **Direct**: Clear, concise, no fluff
- **Friendly**: Warm but professional

### Writing Guidelines
- Use "you" and "your" (second person)
- Active voice over passive
- Short sentences and paragraphs
- Specific numbers over vague claims
- Show, don't tell

### Words to Use
- Save, optimize, discover, reduce
- Free, instant, personalized, simple
- Hidden, wasted, unnecessary, excess
- Smart, data-driven, actionable

### Words to Avoid
- Revolutionary, game-changing, disruptive
- Best, perfect, ultimate, amazing
- Complicated jargon
- Hype and superlatives
