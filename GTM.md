# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Product Positioning

**Tagline**: "Stop Overpaying for AI Tools"

**Value Proposition**: Free AI spend analysis that helps startups discover hidden savings across their AI tool stack in under 2 minutes.

**Target Audience**: 
- Early-stage startups (seed to Series A)
- Engineering teams (5-50 people)
- Technical founders and CTOs
- DevOps and platform teams

## Launch Strategy

### Phase 1: Soft Launch (Week 1-2)

**Goals**:
- Validate product-market fit
- Gather initial feedback
- Fix critical bugs
- Build initial user base (50-100 audits)

**Tactics**:
- Share with personal network
- Post in private Slack communities
- Direct outreach to 20 target users
- Collect detailed feedback

**Success Metrics**:
- 50+ completed audits
- 30%+ lead capture rate
- 10+ pieces of feedback
- < 5% error rate

### Phase 2: Product Hunt Launch (Week 3)

**Goals**:
- Reach #1-3 Product of the Day
- Drive 500+ audits
- Build email list (200+ leads)
- Generate social proof

**Preparation**:
- Create compelling PH listing
- Prepare launch assets
- Line up supporters
- Schedule for Tuesday/Wednesday
- Prepare response templates

**Product Hunt Listing**:
- **Tagline**: "Discover hidden savings in your AI tool spending"
- **Description**: "Free tool that analyzes your AI software costs and finds savings opportunities. Get personalized recommendations for Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, and more in 2 minutes."
- **First Comment**: Share backstory, problem, and ask for feedback

**Launch Day Plan**:
- Post at 12:01 AM PST
- Engage with every comment
- Share on Twitter, LinkedIn
- Email personal network
- Post in communities
- Monitor analytics

**Success Metrics**:
- Top 5 Product of the Day
- 500+ audits completed
- 200+ email captures
- 50+ upvotes
- 20+ comments

### Phase 3: Community Expansion (Week 4-8)

**Goals**:
- Reach 2,000+ audits
- Build community presence
- Generate organic traffic
- Establish thought leadership

**Tactics**:
- Reddit posts (r/startups, r/SaaS, r/entrepreneur)
- Hacker News submission
- Twitter thread about findings
- LinkedIn articles
- Indie Hackers post
- Dev.to article

**Content Strategy**:
- "We analyzed 500 startups' AI spending - here's what we found"
- "5 ways startups waste money on AI tools"
- "The hidden cost of AI tool sprawl"
- Case studies from real audits

**Success Metrics**:
- 2,000+ total audits
- 500+ email list
- 1,000+ organic visitors/week
- 10+ backlinks
